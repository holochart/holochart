Express statistics and catalogue (E23.5, E23.6). `trendline` on `scatter` and `densityContour` draws plotly.py's trendlines — `'ols'` (statsmodels' OLS with `addConstant`, `logX` / `logY`; px's hover header with the equation and R²), `'lowess'` (a port of statsmodels' LOWESS: tricube weights, three robustifying passes, `delta`; matches R's `lowess` on statsmodels' reference data), and pandas' `'rolling'` (windows of points or time spans over dates, `center`, `winType`), `'expanding'` and `'ewm'` (`com` / `span` / `halflife` / `alpha`, `adjust`, `ignoreNa`) with `function` / `functionArgs` and `minPeriods` — per group or once over all rows (`trendlineScope: 'overall'`), with `trendlineOptions` and `trendlineColorOverride`. `getTrendlineResults(figure | chart)` returns the OLS fits (coefficients, R², standard errors, t and p values) per group; they are kept beside the figure, not in its JSON. `agg` on `bar`, `line` and `area` (a Holochart extension) aggregates the rows sharing a position per group: `'sum'`, `'avg'`, `'count'`, `'min'`, `'max'`, `'median'` or a function, titled like a histogram's aggregate. New functions `funnel`, `funnelArea`, `scatterPolar`, `linePolar` (with `lineClose`) and `barPolar` follow `px.funnel`, `px.funnel_area`, `px.scatter_polar`, `px.line_polar` and `px.bar_polar`. `ols`, `lowess`, `rolling`, `expanding` and `ewm` are exported.
