The `indicator` trace (E12.7) in `@mk7s/holochart-traces-finance`: one `value` as a big number (`number.prefix` / `suffix` / `valueformat` / `font`), its delta to `delta.reference` (absolute or `relative`, `position` top / bottom / left / right, ▲ / ▼ symbols and colors, `prefix` / `suffix` / `valueformat`) and / or a gauge (`gauge.shape` `angular` or `bullet`, `bar`, `bgcolor`, `bordercolor`, `borderwidth`, templated `steps`, `threshold`, and an `axis` with the cartesian tick attributes), plus `title`, `align` and `domain` (including `layout.grid` cells). Every mode combination is laid out like Plotly: the number and delta are fitted to the domain when no font size is set (never above 80 px, keeping the smallest scale across redraws), unformatted numbers are rounded like tick labels of a `[0, 1.5 × value]` axis, and `valueformat` goes through Plotly's `adjustFormat`. An angular gauge is one instanced GPU arc set (background, steps, bar, threshold, outline and tick marks) and a bullet gauge one rect set; all text is one SDF batch. `value` and `delta.reference` are animatable: `react` / `animate` with a transition counts the number up, formatted every frame, and sweeps the gauge bar (snapping under reduced motion). Accessible descriptions read the value, its change and the gauge. The default template gives indicators a bright number, the colorway's emerald and red deltas, a faint gauge track and outline, a blue bar, a bright threshold and 8 px tick labels.
