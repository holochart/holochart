Docs: a Dallas weather demo page that charts 87 years of daily readings from Dallas Love Field (NOAA): every daily high and low on one zoomable date axis, the year in days against its records, calendar and month-by-year heatmaps, rain through the year, a wind rose, a 3D temperature landscape, a climate spiral and an animated seasonal curve.
