The plotly.py themes now match plotly.py's template files: `ggplot2` and `seaborn` no longer set `title.x`, `simple_white` no longer sets a tick color, annotation defaults match per theme, and `xgridoff`, `ygridoff`, `gridon` and `presentation` carry plotly.py's axis title standoff and pie `automargin`.
