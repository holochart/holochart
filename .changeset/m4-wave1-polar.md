Polar subplots with `scatterpolar` (E11.4) and `barpolar` (E11.5) traces. `layout.polar`, `polar2`, … (picked with the trace `subplot`) have a `domain` (or `layout.grid` cell), `sector`, `hole`, `bgcolor`, `gridshape` (`'circular'`, or `'linear'` polygons on category angular axes), an angular axis (`linear` or `category`, `direction`, `rotation`, `period`, `thetaunit`) and a radial axis (`linear`, `log`, `date` or `category`, `range` / `autorange` with `rangemode: 'tozero'` by default, `angle`, `side`, `title`), both with the cartesian tick, line and grid attributes. `scatterpolar` takes `r` / `theta` (or `r0` / `dr`, `theta0` / `dtheta`) in `thetaunit` degrees, radians or gradians (plotly.js reads gradians as radians), every scatter mode, marker, text and hover style, `line.shape: 'spline'` (screen-space Catmull-Rom, like Plotly) and `fill: 'toself'` (radar charts) or `'tonext'`; lines and fills are clipped to the subplot's radial range, sector and hole, and markers outside it are hidden. `barpolar` draws annular-sector bars (one instanced draw call per trace; polygon-edged bars on `gridshape: 'linear'`), stacked or overlaid by `polar.barmode` with `polar.bargap`, `base`, `offset`, `width` and bar's marker styles (`marker.pattern` not yet). Hover shows `r: …` / `θ: …` (`%{r}`, `%{theta}` in `hovertemplate`; bars by containment). Drags follow Plotly: the radial axis handle changes the radial range end or the axis angle, the band outside the circle rotates the angular axis, a zoom box (`dragmode: 'zoom'`) sets the radial range and a double-click resets, with `relayouting` / `relayout` using Plotly's keys (`polar.radialaxis.range[1]`, `polar.radialaxis.angle`, `polar.angularaxis.rotation`). The accessible description reads each polar trace by kind (radar, polar line, polar scatter, polar bar) with its lowest and highest `r` and a table of `r` / `θ`. Box and lasso selection on polar subplots are not supported yet, and transitions don't interpolate polar traces (as in Plotly). New exports `polarComponent` (the polar axes and drags; included in `sciTraces`), `polarAttributes` and `PolarSubplot`. Runtime: new `subplotDomain` trace-module hook (traces placed by a layout container's `domain`, drawn and hovered like domain traces), `crossTraceLayout` now runs once per function so modules sharing one are laid out together, and setting `polarN.radialaxis.range` (or one end) with `relayout` turns its `autorange` off like a cartesian range. `@mk7s/holochart-traces-basic` exports `buildLinePath` and `barStyle`. The `holochart` template styles polar subplots and polar traces, and the plotly.py themes carry their polar blocks.
