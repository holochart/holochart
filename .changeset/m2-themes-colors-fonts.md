Themes, colors and fonts (E8.1–E8.3): 15 built-in themes (`holochart`, `holochart-dark`, `high-contrast`, `neon` and plotly.py's `plotly`, `plotly_white`, `plotly_dark`, `simple_white`, `ggplot2`, `seaborn`, `presentation`, `xgridoff`, `ygridoff`, `gridon`, `none`); every plotly.py palette and colorscale (cmocean, CARTO, ColorBrewer, cyclical) with `_r` variants, `colors.register` / `colorways.register`, `layout.colorscale` and `layout.colorscaleInterpolation` (`rgb`, `oklab`, `lab`, `hcl`); `fonts.register('Inter', { regular, bold, italic, boldItalic })` and the `font.variant`, `textcase`, `lineposition` and `shadow` attributes.
