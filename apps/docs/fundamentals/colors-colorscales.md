# Colors, colorscales & colorbars

This page will explain how colors are specified and how numeric data is mapped to color.

Planned topics:

- Color formats: names, hex, `rgb()`, `hsl()`, and alpha
- Colorways and qualitative palettes
- Colorscales: built-in sequential, diverging, and cyclical scales, reversed `_r` variants, and
  custom scales
- `cmin`, `cmax`, `cmid`, and shared color axes (`coloraxis`)
- Colorbars
- Registering your own palettes and colorscales

See also the [colorscales gallery](/reference/colorscales).
